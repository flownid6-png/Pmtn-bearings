INDUSTRY APPLICATION

Bearings for Rolling Mills.

Rolling mills use rotating stands and work rolls to reduce and shape metal stock. Bearing positions can see substantial radial loading, heat, vibration and repeated operating cycles. The right selection depends on the mill design, roll arrangement, shaft dimensions, speed and lubrication plan. Spherical roller and four-row taper roller bearing families are commonly considered for rolling mill equipment. Share the bearing number or machine position with our team so the specification can be checked against your requirement. Maintenance access and operating temperature should also be included when checking a bearing designation.
