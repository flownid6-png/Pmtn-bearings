HGR linear rails provide high-precision linear motion for CNC machines, automation and robotics.

HGR technical features

Accuracy gradeC, H, P, SP, UP
Dynamic load ratingUp to 57,800 N
Static load ratingUp to 85,200 N
MaterialBearing steel (52CrMo / GCr15)
Surface hardnessHRC 58–62 (rail); HRC 58–64 (block)
LubricationGrease or oil
MountingTop or bottom
Operating temperature−20 °C to +80 °C
Application speedUp to 5 m/s
Corrosion protectionOptional nickel or chrome plating

Common applications include CNC machines, industrial automation, robotics, laser cutting, packaging, semiconductor equipment, woodworking and printing.
