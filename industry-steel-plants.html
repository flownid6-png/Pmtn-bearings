INDUSTRY APPLICATION

Bearings for Steel Plants.

Steel plant equipment can involve high loads, elevated temperatures, vibration and continuous production cycles. Conveyors, processing lines and mill stands each place different demands on rotating components. Spherical roller and four-row taper roller bearing families are commonly used in some steel plant applications, subject to the machine design and bearing position. Selection depends on load direction, speed, lubrication, shaft and housing dimensions, and operating environment.
