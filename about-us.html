PREMACH TRANSMISSION

Our story

Premach Transmission was founded in Delhi in 2016 by Late Shri Mohinder Kapila. The company was set up to supply industrial bearings, shafts and linear parts to buyers who need the right product and clear answers.

As an authorised distributor of PMTN and URB Bearings, we supply PMTN Ball Bearings, Spherical Roller Bearings, Cylindrical Roller Bearings, Taper Roller Bearings, Four-Row Taper Roller Bearings, Four-Row Cylindrical Roller Bearings, Spherical Roller Thrust Bearings and Thrust Ball Bearings. PMTN Shafts are available in diameters from 6 mm to 80 mm; we also supply Linear Bearings, Linear Blocks and Linear Rails.

Bearings work inside machines that cannot afford to stop: rolling mills, mining and mineral processing, printing and packaging, gym equipment and many more industries. That is why we start every enquiry with your application, not only a part number.

Our buyers are in India and abroad. Send us a bearing number, a size or a description of your machine, and we confirm availability and price on WhatsApp or email.

Today the company is led by the Kapila family: Mr. Rajinder Kapila, Mr. Tarun Kapila and Mr. Nikhil Kapila. They carry forward the work and the customer relationships built by the founder.
