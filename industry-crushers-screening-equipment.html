INDUSTRY APPLICATION

Bearings for Crushers & Screening Equipment.

Crushers and screening equipment process material through repeated impact, vibration and rotating motion. Bearing positions may experience heavy loads and challenging contamination conditions, so correct mounting, lubrication and maintenance are important. Spherical roller and spherical roller thrust bearing families are commonly used in selected machine arrangements, subject to the equipment design. Bearing choice depends on load direction, shaft and housing fit, speed and operating environment. Share the equipment model and existing bearing designation to discuss the specific requirement. Shaft size, operating speed and maintenance conditions help define the enquiry.
