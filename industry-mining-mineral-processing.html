INDUSTRY APPLICATION

Bearings for Mining & Mineral Processing.

Mining and mineral processing equipment operates in demanding environments that can include dust, impact, vibration and heavy rotating loads. Crushers, screens, conveyors and support rollers each have different bearing positions and operating conditions. Spherical roller bearing families are commonly used in equipment where alignment and load conditions need careful consideration. The suitable bearing depends on the shaft, housing, speed, contamination controls and maintenance practices. Send the equipment type and existing bearing designation to discuss an appropriate product enquiry. Equipment duty, impact levels and contamination controls help narrow the product enquiry.
